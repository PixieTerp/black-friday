Part 1 rules

Percent off = how much you save, out of the starting price.

% off = (old price − new price) ÷ old price × 100

Advertised % off: use the "Was" price on the red tag.

Real % off: use the Nov 1 price on the chart. That is the regular price before the Black Friday hype.

Real deal = the real % off is 10% or more. Less than that is a fake deal.

Round to one decimal place, like 6.7.

Part 2 rules: harder

Real price trackers compare the sale price to the lowest price before the sale. Now you do too.

real % off = (lowest price before − Black Friday price) ÷ lowest price before × 100

If the Black Friday price is higher than the lowest price, your answer is negative. Example: −20 means you paid 20% MORE.

Real deal = 10% or more. Anything else is a fake deal.

What real researchers found

  • A UK consumer group (Which?) checked 175 Black Friday 2024 deals. Every one was the same price or cheaper at some other time within 6 months.
  • A US study (KnownHost) found about 30% of popular tech products got a price hike right before Black Friday, by about 20.5% on average.
  • One 10-inch tablet went from $75 in early November to $139.99 right before Black Friday.
  • But not every deal is fake: in the same study, 42% had steady prices and then a real drop.

Products in this app are simplified versions of real patterns that price trackers reported. Brands are removed and the numbers are rounded to make the math easier.
